🐙 Imaginer 1.14 — The octopus learned a new trick

New in the menu bar
📂 Import
Meet our octopus! It packs your pictures when you download them all. Now it brings pictures in, too.
Click 📂, pick your pictures, done. Drag and drop still works.
More than 100 pictures at once? The octopus asks first. Even eight arms have their limits.
  • 🪟Transparent backgrounds — the new Background dropdown sits in the menu bar. Pick Transparent, and your picture comes without a background, like a sticker.
  • 🎒Download All Images keeps every picture — each one gets its own name, even twins from the same prompt.
  • 🪶Pictures travel light — imported pictures drop their camera data, such as the location. Edits send OpenAI only the picture, without its file name.
  • 🖌️Your mask now really reaches OpenAI — the models still like to paint outside the lines. 🙈
🧹 One question for older galleries — Imaginer now keeps every picture as a clean PNG. If your gallery holds older pictures, it offers to convert them right after this message. Your pictures and prompts stay. Want an exact copy first? The dialog tells you how.
