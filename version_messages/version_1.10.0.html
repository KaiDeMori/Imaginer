Imaginer 1.10 — Flip through your images

Browsing a set of generations no longer means dropping back to the gallery every time. The big viewer now steps from one image to the next, and a couple of keystrokes keep your hands on the prompt.

  • ◀ ▶Navigate without leaving the viewer — arrow buttons step to the previous or next gallery image, and they dim when you reach either end.
  • 🔆You never lose your place — the gallery highlights the image you are viewing and scrolls it into sight as you move.
  • ⌨️Generate from the keyboard — press Ctrl+Enter (or Cmd+Enter) in the prompt box to fire off a generation.
Less clicking around, more making. ✨
