Imaginer 1.11 — Cleaner imports

Dragging in images or reference sets is a little more forgiving now. Bad files get caught before they cause trouble, and a couple of small rough edges got smoothed out.

  • 🖼️Imports are checked up front — unsupported formats, oversized files, and batches that exceed the image limit are caught immediately, with a clear message instead of a silent failure.
  • 🔄Fresh models after an update — the available model list now refreshes automatically once an update finishes.
  • ❓FAQ, one click away — the About dialog now links straight to it.
Fewer surprises, more generating. ✨
