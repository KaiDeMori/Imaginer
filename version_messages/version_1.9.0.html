Imaginer 1.9 - A calmer Configuration dialog

The settings dialog has been reorganized so common choices are easier to find and expert options stay out of the way.

  • ⚙️Four focused tabs - Account, Generation, Files, and Advanced now group settings by what you are trying to do.
  • 📝Longer export filenames - downloaded PNG names now use up to 110 prompt characters by default, configurable up to 230 in Files.
  • 📦File tools together - Download All Images, Refresh Cache, filename length, and Delete Gallery now live under Files.
  • 🧭Cleaner spacing - related settings are visually grouped, and dependent settings such as partial previews now sit where they belong.
A little less hunting, a little more making.
