Imaginer 1.13 — Transparency whenever you want it

Flare and Sunburst both support transparent backgrounds, so the setting works the same whichever one you use. That is why it now sits in the menu bar, where you can reach it between one generation and the next.

  • 🪟Both models support transparency — Flare and Sunburst accept the setting for generations and for edits alike, so your choice carries across both.
  • 🔝Background dropdown in the menu bar — pick Automatic, Transparent, or Opaque next to the model dropdown. Your choice takes effect the moment you pick it.
  • ⚙️The familiar setting still works — Config › Generation › Background changes the same thing, so use whichever you reach for first.
